Jiayi Liu

Assistant Professor · University of Pittsburgh

I am an Assistant Professor in Information Systems and Technology Management at the University of Pittsburgh School of Business. I received my Ph.D. in Information Systems and Operations Management from Emory University.

My research sits at the intersection of healthcare operations, behavioral economics, and health IT. I study how individuals and organizations respond to changes in technology and policy, and how those behavioral responses shape the efficiency and equity of healthcare delivery. My work has appeared in Management Science, Manufacturing & Service Operations Management, Operations Research, Production and Operations Management, and Nature Human Behaviour.
